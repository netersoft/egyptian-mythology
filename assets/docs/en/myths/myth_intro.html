Myths are intended to explain the origins of a belief and they take an important place in ancient Egyptian civilization because the Egyptians of ancient times have sought to interpret all the phenomena they could observe through the prism of beliefs. Thus several great myths emerged as:

  • The myth of the day cycle which describes the struggle that the god Re must wage every night against the forces of chaos to allow the sun to reappear every morning,
  • The Osirian myth whose history revolves around the reign and assassination of the god Osiris and his political consequences.
  • and The myth of death which relates the journey of the deceased to the afterlife and the trials that he must pass through to finally access eternal rest
