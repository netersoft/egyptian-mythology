Myths set out to explain the origins of a belief, and they held an important place in ancient Egyptian civilization, because the Egyptians sought to interpret every phenomenon they could observe through the lens of their beliefs. Several great myths arose in this way:

  • The myth of the day cycle, which describes the battle the god Re must fight every night against the forces of chaos so that the sun can rise again each morning;
  • The Osirian myth, which tells of the reign and murder of the god Osiris and of their political consequences;
  • and the myth of death, which recounts the journey of the deceased to the afterlife and the trials they must overcome before reaching eternal rest.
