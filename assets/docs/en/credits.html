Texts

The texts of this app were written for it and are released under the Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0. They draw on the following ancient sources:

  • the Pyramid Texts and the Coffin Texts;
  • the Book of the Dead and the Amduat;
  • the Book of the Heavenly Cow;
  • the Shabaka Stone (British Museum);
  • the Metternich Stela (Metropolitan Museum of Art);
  • Papyrus Chester Beatty I (The Contendings of Horus and Seth);
  • Herodotus, Histories, book II;
  • Plutarch, On Isis and Osiris.
