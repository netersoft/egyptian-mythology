Explore the gods, myths and creation stories of ancient Egypt, then test what you have learned with a timed quiz. A complete guide to Egyptian mythology in your pocket: it works offline, with no ads and no account.

★ THE GODS OF ANCIENT EGYPT
42 illustrated entries on the gods and goddesses of the Egyptian pantheon: Re (Ra), Osiris, Isis, Horus, Anubis, Thoth, Seth, Bastet, Sekhmet, Hathor, Amun, Ptah, Maat, Sobek, Bes, Apophis and many more. Discover the role of each deity, their symbols, how the Egyptians depicted them and the stories told about them.

★ THE CREATION MYTHS
How did the world begin? Each great religious centre had its own answer. Discover the four Egyptian cosmogonies: Heliopolis and its Ennead, Hermopolis and its Ogdoad, Memphis and the creator god Ptah, Thebes and Amun.

★ THE GREAT MYTHS
• The Osirian myth: the murder of Osiris by Seth, the quest of Isis and the struggle of Horus for the throne of Egypt
• The myth of the day cycle: the journey of the sun god's bark through the underworld and its nightly battle against the serpent Apophis
• The myth of death: mummification, the journey of the soul and the weighing of the heart before Osiris
• The Distant Goddess: the Eye of Re who fled to Nubia, and her return
• The Famine Stela: seven years of famine under King Djoser, and the god Khnum

★ A REFERENCE AT HAND
• A glossary of 40 terms: ka, ba, ankh, Duat, Book of the Dead, Pyramid Texts, canopic jars, uraeus…
• A map of the main cult centres along the Nile, from the Delta to Aswan
• A timeline of ancient Egypt, from the Predynastic Period to the Roman Period
• Search: find a god, a myth or a term in an instant

★ THE QUIZ
• 181 questions on the gods, the cosmogonies and the myths
• Play the full quiz, or only the gods, the cosmogonies or the myths
• 20 seconds per question and 3 lives; 5 correct answers in a row win back a lost life
• The faster you answer, the more points you score
• Review your mistakes: every question has an explanation and a link to the page to read
• Your score history and a progress chart: watch yourself improve, game after game
